Honey Lemon Recipe

Home

Description

This is a delicious recipe to make my very favorite honey lemon drink. It's great for when you're feeling down or a little under the weather. But you don't have to be sick to enjoy honey lemon, it taste great anytime!

Ingredients

  • 2 lemons squeezed
  • 1 litre water
  • 50 grams honey or to taste

Steps

  1. Squeeze and filter the lemon juice into a jar unless you want pulp, in which case just get rid of the seeds.
  2. Warm 1 cup of the water in the coffee mug in the microwave. We'll use this to melt and mix the honey.
  3. Mix the hot water and money together so that all the honey is dissolved.
  4. Mix all the ingredients into the jar thoroughly.
  5. Serve in tall glasses with ice. Or cool in refrigerator to desired coldness before serving.
  6. Enjoy, and share this recipe with your friends!
